## Summary

A summary of the search results is displayed on top. 

![summary](../pics/dpdict.net/dpdict_summary_dark.png#only-dark)
![summary](../pics/dpdict.net/dpdict_summary_light.png#only-light)

Click on the **highlighted word** or the **arrow** to jump to the entry itself.

This summary can be turned off in the settings.

![hide summary](../pics/dpdict.net/dpdict_settings_summary_hide_dark.png#only-dark)
![hide summary](../pics/dpdict.net/dpdict_settings_summary_hide_light.png#only-light)
