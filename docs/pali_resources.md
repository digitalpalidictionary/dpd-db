# Pāḷi Resources

Courses, online dictionaries and reference sites for students of Pāḷi. Several of these dictionaries are also available for offline use on the [Other Dictionaries](other_dicts.md) page.

## Courses

- [DPD Pāḷi Courses](https://digitalpalidictionary.github.io/dpd-pali-courses/){target="_blank"}: the Beginner Pāḷi Course and the Intermediate Pāḷi Course, with exercises and answer keys.

## Pāḷi Dictionaries

- [A Dictionary of Pāli](https://gandhari.org/dictionary?section=dop){target="_blank"} by Margaret Cone, searchable on Gandhari.org.
- [Critical Pāli Dictionary](https://cpd.uni-koeln.de/){target="_blank"} (CPD), the online edition hosted by the University of Cologne.
- [Dictionary of Pāli Proper Names](https://ancient-buddhist-texts.net/Textual-Studies/DPPN/index.htm){target="_blank"} by G. P. Malalasekera, revised by Ven. Ānandajoti.

## Sanskrit Dictionaries

- [Cologne Digital Sanskrit Dictionaries](https://www.sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/){target="_blank"}: more than 40 Sanskrit dictionaries, including Monier-Williams, Apte and Edgerton's Buddhist Hybrid Sanskrit Dictionary.
- [The Sanskrit Heritage Site](https://sanskrit.inria.fr/){target="_blank"}: Gérard Huet's Sanskrit-French dictionary and an edition of Monier-Williams, both linked to grammatical tools such as inflection tables.

## Reference

- [Wisdom Library](https://www.wisdomlib.org/){target="_blank"}: definitions of Buddhist, Hindu and Jain terms gathered from many dictionaries and texts.
