# ePitaka

The full version of DPD is built into the ePitaka app. This is the app that I personally use for my daily Pāḷi reading and study. It shows the Pāḷi and a translation side by side, sentence by sentence, in over 20 languages. Tap any word and the full DPD entry opens right next to the text.

## ePitaka contains

- the full Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana Tipiṭaka, with commentaries
- translations in over 20 languages, including Myanmar Nissaya
- the full DPD, with the deconstructor and grammar dictionary
- Pāḷi in 18 scripts
- fast search across all books
- highlights, notes and bookmarks
- read-aloud with word-by-word highlighting
- AI study tools

Everything works offline, except DPD's audio pronunciation and the AI tools.

## Translations

You can read the Pāḷi side by side with a translation in any of these languages:

Bengali, Chinese, English, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Japanese, Khmer, Korean, Lao, Myanmar, Myanmar Nissaya, Nepali, Portuguese, Russian, Sinhala, Spanish, Tamil, Thai and Vietnamese.

Download the ones you want from inside the app.

## Looking Up a Word

To look a word up in the dictionary, simply tap on it. On desktop, DPD opens in a panel next to the text. On a phone, it slides up from the bottom of the screen.

![looking up a word on a phone](../pics/epitaka/lookup-phone-light.png#only-light){width="360"}
![looking up a word on a phone](../pics/epitaka/lookup-phone-dark.png#only-dark){width="360"}

The entry has buttons to open inflection tables, roots, families, frequency and sutta examples. Click the speaker to hear the word pronounced.

If the word is a compound or sandhi, ePitaka shows how DPD's deconstructor splits it.

You can also type a word into the dictionary panel's search box.

## Choose How Much to Show

DPD in ePitaka has three levels of detail.

- **Saṅkhitta** (Brief) shows just the summary line.
- **Majjhima** (Standard) shows the summary with buttons for more. This is the default.
- **Vitthāra** (Full) shows everything.

## Get The Latest Version of DPD

ePitaka checks for a new DPD release when the app starts. When there is one, a notice pops up with a button to download it.

If your connection drops during the download, it picks up again from where it stopped.

## AI Study Tools

ePitaka also has AI tools for studying the texts.

- **Vīmaṃsā** answers your questions about what you are reading. Each answer has links back to the passages it used.
- **Gavesanā** searches the Tipiṭaka for whatever you describe in your own words.
- **Translation Builder** lets you make a new translation of the Tipiṭaka into your own language.

The translations in the app are made with the Translation Builder.

⚠️ Always double-check AI responses. AI can be completely wrong while sounding confident. Treat its answers and translations as a study aid, and check them against the Pāḷi.
