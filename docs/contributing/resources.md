# Help with Resources

You can offer your time, your energy, or a service that you buy yourself, such as a server or subscription.

To make an offer, use the [contribution form](index.md#offer-your-help).

The maintainers do not deal with money. No money is sent to DPD, and donations are not accepted.

If you don't have time to contribute directly, you can still help by sharing DPD with fellow Pāḷi students and using it in your projects.
